# Business Requirements

## Objective

Provide management and operational teams with a single view of clearing activity and transaction performance.

## Main Questions

1. How many transactions were processed?
2. What is the total transaction value?
3. What is the net clearing position by payment system?
4. How much commission revenue was generated?
5. Are transactions successfully reconciled?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
Headline KPIs, trends, and payment system comparison.

### 2. Transaction Analytics
Volume and amount analysis by channel, type, and date.

### 3. Clearing & Settlement
Incoming/outgoing flows and net clearing positions.

### 4. Operational Monitoring
Reconciliation rate and exception monitoring.
